Product relevance

DataWeave is named in 48% of AI answers to its 24 tracked buyer prompts, more often than any other vendor the engines name.

Forty days of daily runs on ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, 2,880 answers in total, read for whether DataWeave was named, whether dataweave.com was cited, which other vendors appeared, how DataWeave was framed, and which 32,874 sources the engines drew on. The two filters above apply to every chart and table that recomputes from the answers.

Prepared for Shai, Global Marketing. Runs from 30 Aug to 8 Oct 2026, United States. Analysis 8 Oct 2026.

The short version

The findings below describe the full set of 24 prompts. The figures and charts from the next block onward respond to the filters.

  1. DataWeave is the most-named vendor on this prompt set, and the lead is real. Counting every vendor name in the answer text, not only the competitors HubSpot tracks, DataWeave appears in 48% of 2,880 answers. Intelligence Node is next at 35%, then Profitero 32%, Competera 27%, Wiser 24%, Salsify 21%. The lead holds on every engine, in every prompt category except the AI prompts (where Semrush edges it), and in both halves of the period. The product view in the next finding is the important qualification.
  2. Visibility is uneven across engines, and the gap is about sources, not sentiment. ChatGPT names DataWeave in 63% of answers and reads dataweave.com directly (8% of everything it cites). Perplexity and Gemini name it in 40% and read the site almost never (1% of citations each). They build answers from third-party listicles and directories, so DataWeave only appears when those lists include it.
  3. Four prompts earn nothing. The three AI content and AI visibility prompts and "alternatives to NielsenIQ" return DataWeave in 0 to 1% of 480 answers. The AI prompts are answered from Google, Semrush, Ahrefs, Yotpo and Otterly sources; NielsenIQ alternatives are answered from CB Insights, Craft and SourceForge profiles where DataWeave is not listed.
  4. When DataWeave is named, it is a headline pick, not a footnote. 92% of mentions are a bold entry, table row or heading. Median position is third among the vendors named, first in 21% of answers, top three in 60%. On differentiator prompts it is named first 65% of the time. It appears in the closing recommendation of 29% of all answers, against 20% for the next vendor.
  5. By product line, the lead is a composite and the picture is different. On the ten pricing intelligence prompts DataWeave is third: Intelligence Node 65%, Competera 63%, DataWeave 58%. On the ten digital shelf prompts Profitero leads 53% to 50%. On the five content optimization prompts DataWeave is fifth at 24% behind Salsify, Akeneo, Syndigo and Semrush. DataWeave ties for first on assortment (with Intelligence Node, 45%) and data collection (with Bright Data, 51%). The overall lead comes from being named across every category while each rival is strong in one.
  6. The engines describe DataWeave as enterprise pricing and assortment intelligence. In 666 comparison-table rows, the words "intelligence", "pricing", "assortment", "competitive" and "enterprise" dominate; "product matching" appears in 51 rows and "content" in 33. The positioning the engines have learned is narrower than the product line.
  7. Mention rate fell in the last two weeks. 50% in the first four weeks, 42% in the last twelve days. ChatGPT held at 62 to 64%. Perplexity dropped from 44% to 32% and Gemini from 43% to 33%, concentrated on the pricing-provider, agentic-data and most-accurate-data prompts. Competitors did not gain; the lists got shorter.
  8. The result is specific to this prompt set. Six prompts ask for alternatives to a named competitor and four use DataWeave's own vocabulary. On the eight generic category prompts the lead is narrower: DataWeave 47%, Intelligence Node 36%, Salsify 35%. This is share of voice on DataWeave's buying conversations in the US, not a market-wide measurement.

What to do with this

Eight blocks, in the order to run them. The outreach, protect and directory tables recompute from the filters so each product or category owner can pull their own list.

Immediate next steps, this week

  1. Canonicalise the hosts. 209 citations in this window point at next.dataweave.com and stage.dataweave.com. Set canonical tags to production and block the staging host. Engines that cite a preview URL today will drop it when it stops resolving, and the citation moves nowhere unless the production page is the canonical.
  2. Collapse the regional copies. The product matching, DSA, pricing intelligence and vs-Wiser pages each exist at two to four regional paths. One canonical URL per page, with the others 301'd, consolidates the signal the engines already send.
  3. Claim and complete the eight directory profiles below. They are free, they decide the displacement answers on Perplexity and Gemini, and the NielsenIQ gap is entirely a directory gap. Same description, same category tags, same screenshots across all of them.
  4. Send the top five outreach pitches. Nimble (two lists), Yieldigo, inriver and Thunderbit between them are cited 850 times in the selection and already decide most Gemini and Perplexity answers on pricing and DSA. Three already list DataWeave; ask for position and copy. Two do not; pitch with a data point.
  5. Add six vendors to HubSpot tracking. Salsify, NielsenIQ, Syndigo, Omnia Retail, Bright Data, Semrush. The tracked set currently omits the vendors that beat DataWeave on content and pricing prompts.

Ninety-day sequence

  1. Weeks 1 to 2. Hygiene and protect. Canonicals, regional consolidation, the eight directory profiles, and position and copy requests to the pages that already list DataWeave.
  2. Weeks 2 to 6. Earn. Pitch the outreach targets above, highest citations first, leading with a data point rather than a listing request. Run the G2 and Gartner Peer Insights review campaign in parallel; 20 to 30 fresh reviews changes category placement.
  3. Weeks 3 to 8. Build. The five versus pages first (highest intent, smallest effort), then the three AI-surface pages, then the content optimization pages and the pricing hub.
  4. Weeks 6 to 10. Publish the three stats pages and seed them to the aggregators.
  5. Weeks 8 to 12. Pay selectively: G2 premium, AIMultiple, Gartner Digital Markets PPC, in that order.
  6. Ongoing. Measure as below.

How to measure it

Report four numbers monthly, by product line

Mention rate, site-cited rate, share of voice against the full vendor set, and position when named. Report them per product line (the filter above) rather than blended, because the blended number is high while pricing and content are behind.

Watch Perplexity and Gemini separately

ChatGPT held at 62 to 64% through the window; the decline came from Perplexity (44% to 32%) and Gemini (43% to 33%). Both read third-party lists, so the outreach and directory work above is what moves them. If those two do not recover within a month of the outreach landing, the lists were refreshed without DataWeave and the protect step needs repeating.

Method and caveats

  • Source: HubSpot AI-visibility prompt-run export. 24 buyer prompts, three engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini), one run per prompt per engine per day from 30 Aug to 8 Oct 2026, US location. 2,880 answers.
  • Prompt category follows the tags in the tracked prompt list: category discovery (8), competitor displacement (6), differentiator (4), AI capabilities and surfaces (6).
  • Product relevance is this review's own mapping of each prompt to DataWeave's product line (Pricing Intelligence, Assortment Analytics, Digital Shelf Analytics, Content Optimization, Data Collection), based on the capability the prompt asks about. A prompt can map to more than one product. It does not use HubSpot's product field.
  • Mention and site-cited flags are HubSpot's and were verified against the answer text. Share of voice counts every vendor name in the answer body with citation links stripped, so it is not limited to HubSpot's tracked competitor list.
  • Citations are every URL the engine returned with its answer, counted once per answer. The same page cited in 40 daily answers counts 40 times. Domains are rolled up to their root.
  • Position and framing are derived from the response text with pattern matching. They are directional, not exact.
  • Controller categories (competitor, adjacent, directory, community, third-party) follow the Master Tag List from the cited-source strategy workbook, extended to domains that appear only in this export.
  • Daily runs sample what the engines said that day. Answers vary run to run, which is why the review reads rates over 40 days rather than any single result.
